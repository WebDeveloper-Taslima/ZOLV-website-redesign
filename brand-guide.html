DELIVERABLE 01 // LOGO REDESIGN

THE REFINED ZOLV WORDMARK

The Brief Requirement: "Improve the existing ZOLV wordmark while retaining its recognizable personality. Explore a more refined, balanced and premium execution. Any dissolving or dripping detail should be subtle and intentional."

Previous Concept Diagnosis

The previous prototype (shown below from client upload) featured heavy liquid dripping teardrops hanging from the foot of 'L' and an inflated balloon aesthetic. While playful, it lacked the razor precision of an elite lifestyle biohacking brand.

1. Kinetic 45° Speed Terminal Cuts

The letter Z features a forward-thrust 45-degree speed chamfer, evoking speed of onset (instant sublingual absorption) and athletic energy akin to Red Bull.

2. Subtle & Intentional Sublingual Dissolve

Rather than messy melting drips that blur at small sizes, the dissolve effect is executed as an intentional 4-stage micro-particle dispersion off the apex of the letter V. This symbolizes the oral film dissolving into nanometer bioactives on the tongue.
